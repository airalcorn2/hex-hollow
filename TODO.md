# Hex Hollow outcomes

- [x] Playable top-down witch exploration: a simple SNES Zelda-inspired 2D presentation, directional movement, a spooky Halloween environment with traversable ground, boundaries and obstacles.
- [x] Spell ingredient loop: six distinct items can be found and collected, carried back, and deposited in a central cauldron; depositing every ingredient completes the spell with visible magical feedback.
- [x] Clear game flow: English title and concise controls, readable recipe checklist and interaction cues, pause/resume, completion screen and replay. In-progress play saves locally.
- [x] Integrated original artwork: readable witch animation, ingredient sprites, cauldron states, cohesive environment and completed-spell effect with clean alpha.
- [ ] Managed playable handoff: game-specific recoverable loading screen, sharing cover and favicon, finite native core-loop evidence, prepared Web export, verified pack, and recorded checkpoint.
- [x] Mooncorn Maze environmental interactions: the well, lantern tree, and pumpkin wagon offer rotating witchy flavor text through E / Space, have visible question-mark indicators and contextual prompts, and can be inspected from reachable paths without changing treat collection or cauldron progression.
- [x] Hollow mask-based collisions: apply the maze's Gaussian edge-density walkable-mask process, small feet-sized footprint, swept movement and corner assistance to Level 1; preserve the artwork, water and cauldron obstacles, all ingredient/companion/landmark interactions, shuffled recipe order, saved progress and CauldronBridge integration; verify reachable approaches from the spawn.
- [x] Cauldron clearing terrain: make the entire central clearing and its decorative weed ring walkable, preserving only the cauldron and companion witches as solid props and leaving the rest of the Hollow's terrain collisions unchanged.
- [x] Portable GitHub distribution: version all game assets, collision masks, fonts, source artwork, import sidecars and license notices in airalcorn2/hex-hollow; exclude caches, builds, dependencies and secrets; preserve the managed Preview binding and provide an asset-preserving sync command for future releases; verify native gameplay from a fresh self-contained checkout without asset downloads.
